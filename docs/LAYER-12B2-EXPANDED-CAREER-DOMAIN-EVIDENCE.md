# Layer 12B2 — Expanded Career-domain evidence

Layer 12B2 is an additive static-natal extension to the existing H10-primary Career overlay. It consumes only supplied Layer 12A facts and performs no calculation, interpretation, timing, scoring, or prediction.

## Approved scope

- H2 is selected as a neutral Career resource/material-context house.
- H11 is selected as a neutral Career gains/acquisition-context house.
- H10 behavior, its limited D10 Rashi context, and its limited Ashtakavarga/Pinda context are unchanged.

The classical house significations and the Career-domain selection are recorded separately in relation facts and provenance. H2 never means salary or compensation. H11 never means promotion or advancement.

## Structural facts only

For H2 and H11, the overlay may preserve supplied Layer 5A house/lord/occupant facts, Layer 5B state facts, Layer 6 Drishti facts, and a supplied selected-house-lord placement in another selected Career-domain house. It neither calculates nor evaluates those facts.

H6, D10 expansion, Ashtakavarga expansion, Dasha, Gochar, transit events, co-activation, outcomes, and all numerical ranking remain outside this layer.

## Data guarantees

Relations have stable identities from the Career ruleset, type, endpoints, and sorted upstream node IDs. Identical relations deduplicate; conflicting factual identities reject. Results are lexicographically ordered and deeply frozen. Missing facts create no inferred relationship and are never negative evidence.
